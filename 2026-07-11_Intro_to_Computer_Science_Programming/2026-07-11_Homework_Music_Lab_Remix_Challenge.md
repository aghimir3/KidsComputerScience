**Date assigned:** July 11, 2026

## Open the Assignment

1. Sign in to Code.org using the class instructions.
2. Join the class section using the code shared by your teacher.

## Your Remix Mission

- Finish the guided Music Lab levels. **30 points**
- Use at least three sounds in your final remix. **20 points**
- Add an AI-generated drum beat. **20 points**
- Make at least two creative changes. **20 points**
- Give your remix a school-appropriate title and test it. **10 points**

## Bonus Challenge (5 points)

Add one extra feature beyond the required steps. Examples include another
sound, a new section, a function, or a surprising change in the music.
