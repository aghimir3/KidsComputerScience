# Classwork: Programming Launch

**Date:** July 11, 2026
**Points:** 100 points + 10 bonus points

## Part 1 - Meet the Class (20 points)

Participate in the whole-class icebreaker. Share only information you are
comfortable sharing. Your location should be a city/state or country, never a
street address.

## Part 2 - Find Your Way in Teams (20 points)

Locate the Saturday meeting in Calendar and identify the purpose of the
Announcements, Classwork, Homework, and Hangout Session channels.

## Part 3 - Code Crew Breakout (20 points)

Join your assigned breakout room, complete your Code Crew role, help your
teammates, and return to the main room when called.

## Part 4 - Create in Code.org (30 points)

Open Music Lab, complete the introductory steps, create a mix with at least two
sounds, and try the AI-generated drums when available.

## Part 5 - Reflection (10 points)

Answer in a complete sentence: What did you learn about programming or teamwork
today?

## Bonus - Imagine the Next Feature (10 points)

Describe one new feature you would add to Music Lab and explain how AI could
help with it.
