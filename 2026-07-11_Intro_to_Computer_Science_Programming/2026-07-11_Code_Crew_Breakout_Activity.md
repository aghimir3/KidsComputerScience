# Code Crew Breakout Activity

**Date:** July 11, 2026
**Format:** Microsoft Teams breakout rooms with three students per room
**Activity:** Code.org Music Lab: Jam Session
**Student work time:** About 35 minutes

## Student Post

Copy the block below into Teams before opening the breakout rooms.

---

## Code Crew Mission

Your breakout room is now a **Code Crew**. Everyone should open Code.org Music
Lab on their own computer:

https://code.org/en-US/hour-of-code/music

### Choose Your First Roles

- **Captain:** keeps the crew on the mission
- **Coach:** helps when someone gets stuck
- **Reporter:** remembers one discovery to share

Change roles after about 10 minutes. Everyone should have a turn helping.

### Crew Goals

1. Make sure all three students open Music Lab.
2. Complete the introductory challenges at your own pace.
3. Create a mix with at least two sounds.
4. Try an AI-generated drum beat when it becomes available.
5. Help every person make visible progress.
6. Choose one discovery or project to share with the class.

### Good Code Crew Habits

- Ask before taking over someone else's work.
- Explain your idea instead of only giving the answer.
- It is okay to make mistakes and try again.
- Keep music at a comfortable volume or use headphones.
- Celebrate when a teammate solves a problem.

### Before You Return

The Reporter should be ready to finish this sentence:

> Our crew discovered that coding can ____.

---

## Teacher Logistics

### Breakout Setup

- Auto-assign or preassign three students per room.
- Keep new students with patient, supportive classmates when possible.
- Post the activity link in the meeting chat before opening rooms.
- Let students know they can use **Ask for help**.

### Broadcast Messages

At about 20 minutes remaining:

> Code Crew check-in: make sure everyone has a working project. Rotate roles.

At about 5 minutes remaining:

> Five minutes left. Choose one discovery or project to share.

At about 1 minute remaining:

> Wrap up your current step. Returning to the main room soon.

### Backup Plans

- **Two students:** one student is Captain/Reporter and one is Coach; swap.
- **One student:** move the student into another room or keep them with a teacher.
- **No audio:** use Code.org Hello World at https://code.org/hello.
- **Access problem:** one student shares a screen while the group makes choices.

## Discussion Prompts

Use one or two after students return:

- What happened when the blocks were in a different order?
- What did your crew do when someone got stuck?
- How did the AI-generated beat change the music?
- What would you add if you had ten more minutes?
