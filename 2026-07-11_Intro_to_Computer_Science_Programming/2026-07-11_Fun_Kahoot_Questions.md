# Fun Practice Kahoot

**Date:** July 11, 2026
**Purpose:** Help new students learn how to play Kahoot
**Questions:** 15
**Suggested time:** 20 seconds per question

## Questions

**Q1. Which planet do we live on?**
A) Mars
B) Earth
C) Venus
D) Jupiter
**Correct: B**

**Q2. What is 5 + 7?**
A) 10
B) 11
C) 12
D) 13
**Correct: C**

**Q3. Which animal is known for black and white stripes?**
A) Zebra
B) Giraffe
C) Elephant
D) Dolphin
**Correct: A**

**Q4. Which device can move the pointer on a computer?**
A) Speaker
B) Printer
C) Mouse
D) Microphone
**Correct: C**

**Q5. What color can you make by mixing blue and yellow?**
A) Purple
B) Orange
C) Pink
D) Green
**Correct: D**

**Q6. How many minutes are in one hour?**
A) 30
B) 45
C) 60
D) 100
**Correct: C**

**Q7. Which is the largest planet in our solar system?**
A) Earth
B) Saturn
C) Mars
D) Jupiter
**Correct: D**

**Q8. What number comes next: 2, 4, 6, 8, ___?**
A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12
**Correct: B**

**Q9. Which game is famous for blocks, crafting, and Creepers?**
A) Minecraft
B) Rocket League
C) Tetris
D) Mario Kart
**Correct: A**

**Q10. Which keyboard key usually starts a new line?**
A) Shift
B) Tab
C) Enter
D) Spacebar
**Correct: C**

**Q11. What does WWW stand for?**
A) World Wide Web
B) Web World Window
C) Worldwide Wireless
D) Wide Web Workspace
**Correct: A**

**Q12. Which is a renewable source of energy?**
A) Coal
B) Sunlight
C) Gasoline
D) Natural gas
**Correct: B**

**Q13. What is frozen water called?**
A) Steam
B) Rain
C) Ice
D) Fog
**Correct: C**

**Q14. Which tool can create text from a written prompt?**
A) Calculator
B) Stopwatch
C) File explorer
D) AI assistant
**Correct: D**

**Q15. What matters most in today's practice Kahoot?**
A) Getting every answer perfect
B) Learning the game and having fun
C) Finishing before everyone else
D) Memorizing your final score
**Correct: B**
