# July 11, 2026 - Teacher Run of Show

## Class Goal

By the end of class, students should feel welcome, know how the class works,
navigate the main Microsoft Teams spaces, use a Teams breakout room, create in
Code.org Music Lab, and know that TypeScript begins next week.

## Before Class

- Open the new July 11 presentation.
- Create the fun Kahoot from `2026-07-11_Fun_Kahoot_Import.xlsx`.
- Create the closing Kahoot from `2026-07-11_Closing_Kahoot_Import.xlsx`.
- Create or select the Code.org class section.
- Assign **Music Lab: Jam Session** from the Hour of Code catalog.
- Replace all bracketed placeholders in the Teams posts.
- Prepare Teams breakout rooms with three students per room.
- Keep the no-audio backup ready: https://code.org/hello

## Adaptive Pacing

The student introduction round is the pacing variable. Do not rush a student
who is speaking. If the round ends early, begin the fun Kahoot before break or
use the extra time for the Teams tour. Keep the break and typing blocks fixed.

## Student Icebreaker

Display the sentence frame and call on students one at a time:

> My name is ____. I live in ____. I am in grade ____. My favorite school
> subject is ____. My favorite hobby is ____.

For privacy, students should share only a city/state or country, never a street
address. Passing on any question is always allowed.

## Teams Tour Checklist

Demonstrate these live instead of relying only on the slide:

1. Open **Calendar** and locate the Saturday class meeting.
2. Open the Kids Computer Science Team.
3. Show **Announcements** for teacher updates.
4. Show **Classwork** for work completed during class.
5. Show **Homework** for after-class assignments.
6. Show **Hangout Session** for 4:30-5:30 PM Pacific help sessions.

## Kahoot Notes

- The first quiz is a game tutorial, not an academic test.
- Remind students to use a recognizable, school-appropriate nickname.
- Read each question aloud for younger students.
- Celebrate participation rather than only the leaderboard.
- The closing quiz should be discussed after each answer.

## Code Crew Notes

- Every student works on their own Code.org project.
- Group members talk, help, and celebrate progress.
- Broadcast a 20-minute check-in and a 5-minute warning.
- If a room has two students, combine Captain and Reporter.
- If Code.org audio is a problem, use Hello World at https://code.org/hello.

## Closing Script

"Today you met your class, practiced Teams, played Kahoot, worked in a
breakout room, and created with Code.org. Next week we begin TypeScript and
start writing our own code. Help is available in the Hangout Session channel
from 4:30 to 5:30 PM Pacific."
